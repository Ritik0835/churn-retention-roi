# Retention ROI Assumptions

These are illustrative planning assumptions, not observed business
results. Replace them with company-specific data when available.

| Assumption | Value / Range | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Offer cost per customer | $10 | Estimated cost of one retention offer |
| Offer success rate — low | 10% | Conservative scenario |
| Offer success rate — base | 20% | Base scenario |
| Offer success rate — high | 30% | Optimistic scenario |
| Months of revenue kept — short | 3 | Short retention scenario |
| Months of revenue kept — base | 6 | Base retention scenario |
| Months of revenue kept — long | 12 | Long retention scenario |

## Interpretation

- Offer success rate means the assumed share of targeted customers
  successfully retained because of the offer.
- MonthlyCharges is used as a revenue proxy, not verified profit.
- The model does not establish that an offer caused retention.
- ROI calculations must account for offer costs and should avoid
  treating all predicted churn as preventable.
