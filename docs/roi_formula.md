# Expected Value per Customer

## Formula

Expected Value per Customer =
Churn Probability × Offer Success Rate × Value Kept
− Offer Cost per Customer

## Variables

- Churn Probability: Model-predicted probability that the customer churns.
- Offer Success Rate: Assumed probability that the retention offer saves
  a customer who would otherwise churn.
- Value Kept: MonthlyCharges × Months of Revenue Kept.
- Offer Cost per Customer: Estimated cost of providing the offer.

## Example

Assume:
- Churn probability = 80%
- Offer success rate = 20%
- Monthly charges = $80
- Months of revenue kept = 6
- Offer cost = $10

Value Kept = $80 × 6 = $480

Expected Value = 0.80 × 0.20 × $480 − $10
Expected Value = $66.80 per targeted customer

## Important Assumptions

- Values are illustrative, not measured business results.
- MonthlyCharges is a revenue proxy, not verified profit.
- The formula assumes the offer only creates value when it saves a
  customer who would otherwise churn.
- This is expected value, not guaranteed value.
- A more complete ROI analysis should consider contribution margin,
  campaign eligibility, and whether retention is actually caused by
  the offer.
